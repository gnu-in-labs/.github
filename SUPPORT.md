# Support

GNU.IN work is experimental and becoming public in stages. Support is best-effort and should not be read as a production support commitment.

## Where to ask

- **Bugs and concrete problems:** open an issue on the affected repository using the bug template.
- **Ideas and improvements:** open an issue using the feature template.
- **General questions:** email [admin@gnu6.live](mailto:admin@gnu6.live).
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue. Follow the [security policy](./SECURITY.md) and email [security@gnu6.live](mailto:security@gnu6.live).

## What helps

Reports are easier to act on when they include:

- the repository or public surface affected;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened;
- logs, commands, screenshots, or environment details when relevant;
- whether the problem touches security, automation, shell execution, or live runtime behavior.

## What not to expect

- guaranteed response times;
- support for undocumented local installs, forks, or private runtime modifications;
- production-readiness commitments for experimental repositories.
